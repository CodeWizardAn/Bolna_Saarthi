Description:
Saarthi is an autonomous multi-agent platform designed for Indian B2B suppliers, EPC contractors, and distributors responding to complex government and PSU tenders (GeM, NTPC, PGCIL, State Discoms). It autonomously deconstructs 80–200+ page bilingual tender PDFs, matches technical specifications against 650+ verified OEM SKUs, applies deterministic physics formulas and statutory IEEMA price variation curves, audits against Indian Standards (IS 694/1554/7098) and GFR 2017 Rule 170, and generates compliant Form F-4 deviation schedules and commercial bids in under 3 minutes.
